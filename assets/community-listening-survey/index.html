Listening Survey

What does our community make harder than it should be—and what would a respectful, useful place offer?

Your responses will help inform the early development of House of Saturn, a proposed community hub connected to the broader Miracle Sun vision.

Before you begin: This is a voluntary listening survey—not an emergency, housing, medical, legal, employment, or acting-placement service. Please do not include Social Security numbers, case numbers, diagnoses, immigration details, account information, names of children, or other sensitive identifying information.
Community listening

Share only what feels safe to share.

The survey is hosted securely through GoHighLevel. Your answers help Miracle Sun listen for common barriers and understand what a future House of Saturn project could responsibly explore. We are also listening for groups that may have fewer safe and constructive avenues for connection or opportunity, including men and adult men seeking mentorship, creative outlets, workforce networks, or supportive community spaces.

Important boundary: House of Saturn is proposed, unfunded, and not yet incorporated. It cannot currently promise housing, emergency response, legal or medical services, job placement, talent representation, acting work, money, or income. Completing the survey does not give permission to record or publish your story.
