01 / The Work

The wound was never a character flaw. It was a survival strategy with nowhere else to go.

Call it the mother wound, the masculine wound, generational pain. Different names, same mechanic. You learn what keeps you safe in the room you were born into. Then you carry it into every room after.

This is not a framework to think your way out of your life. It is a place to be real long enough that the performance loosens and what is already true can finally speak.

“The wound does not check who you are. It looks for someone who has already done the work, so it can finally land somewhere safe.”

One-to-one recovery work

The Lantern

For the thing you have carried so long it started introducing itself as your personality. We identify what is actually happening underneath the story, trace the pattern, and make room for what you were trained to abandon.

For women who were never allowed to be hard. For men who were never allowed to be soft. For anybody done performing strength in a room that never taught them what to do with pain.

Practical document literacy

Navegante

Know what you have before you spend a dime. Navegante is practical support for reading through documents, letters, bills, notices, and systems that were designed to make people feel small and confused.

We slow it down, name what is in front of you, and help you make your next move with your eyes open. No jargon parade. No pretending you should have already known.

Document literacy and practical support only. Navegante is not legal advice or legal representation.
